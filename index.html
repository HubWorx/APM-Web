FAQs, insights, and resources for your APM journey

What's the best starting point for a greenfield APM implementation?

Greenfield

Start with machine health monitoring on critical assets. Deploy IoT sensors, establish data pipelines, then layer in predictive analytics. Build the ISO 55000 framework in parallel to ensure governance scales with technology.

How do I build the business case for executive buy-in?

Greenfield

Focus on ROCE improvement and maintenance cost reduction. Use industry benchmarks (15-40% maintenance savings) and peer case studies. Start with a pilot on high-value assets to demonstrate quick wins within 90 days.

Should I hire internal expertise or use consultants?

Greenfield

Hybrid approach works best. Use consultants for initial strategy and vendor selection, but build internal capability for ongoing operations. Key roles: Data Engineer, Reliability Engineer, and APM Program Manager.

What data infrastructure do I need before starting?

Greenfield

Minimum requirements: historian for time-series data, asset registry (even in Excel initially), and cloud connectivity. Modern platforms like Cognite or Databricks can accelerate this with pre-built industrial data models.

How do I prioritize which assets to monitor first?

Greenfield

Use criticality ranking: production impact × failure probability × detection difficulty. Start with rotating equipment (pumps, compressors, turbines) that have high failure costs and established ML models available.

I'm using Augury/Seeq/Cognite - how do I scale beyond the pilot?

Disruptive

Document pilot ROI meticulously, create internal champions, and develop a rollout playbook. Most scaling failures come from lack of change management - invest in training and workflow integration before expanding.

How do I integrate multiple point solutions into a cohesive strategy?

Disruptive

Use an Industrial DataOps layer (like HighByte) to normalize data across tools. Establish a single source of truth for asset hierarchy. Map each tool's output to ISO 55000 decision frameworks.

My predictive models work but recommendations aren't being actioned - why?

Disruptive

This is the adoption gap. Recommendations lack context (criticality, risk, business impact). Solution: integrate with work management, add health scores, and provide clear prioritization. The holistic approach addresses this directly.

Should I consolidate vendors or maintain best-of-breed?

Disruptive

Best-of-breed typically outperforms suites for specialized use cases. The key is strong data integration. Evaluate: Does the suite genuinely excel at each capability, or just check boxes?

How do I measure true ROI beyond vendor claims?

Disruptive

Track: avoided failures (with $ impact), maintenance labor hours saved, parts inventory reduction, and production uptime gains. Use control groups where possible. Require 6-month baseline before deployment.

What's the role of generative AI in APM?

Disruptive

GenAI excels at: natural language queries on asset data, automated report generation, root cause analysis summaries, and maintenance procedure generation. It complements (not replaces) physics-based and ML models.

My SAP PM/Maximo investment is significant - do I need to replace it?

Legacy

No. EAM systems remain valuable for work management and asset registry. Layer modern APM tools on top for analytics and prediction. Use APIs to push recommendations back into your existing workflow.

How do I extract value from decades of historian data?

Legacy

Historical data is gold for ML training. Use tools like Seeq to analyze patterns, identify failure signatures, and build predictive models. Data quality matters more than quantity - focus on cleaning key asset data first.

We tried ISO 55000 and it stalled - what went wrong?

Legacy

Common failure modes: too much upfront documentation, no quick wins, lack of executive sponsorship. The holistic approach starts with technology wins (months 1-3) to build momentum, then adds governance progressively.

How do I modernize without disrupting operations?

Legacy

Run new systems in parallel initially. Start with read-only analytics before enabling automated actions. Use shadow mode for predictions - compare to actual outcomes before trusting the models.

Our maintenance team is resistant to AI - how do I drive adoption?

Legacy

Position AI as augmenting expertise, not replacing it. Involve technicians in model validation - their domain knowledge improves accuracy. Celebrate early wins publicly. Make the tools genuinely easier than spreadsheets.

What's the typical migration timeline from legacy to modern APM?

Legacy

18-24 months for full transformation. Months 1-6: pilot and prove value. Months 7-12: scale to critical assets. Months 13-18: enterprise rollout. Months 19-24: optimization and continuous improvement.

What's the difference between APM, EAM, and CMMS?

General

CMMS: Work order management. EAM: Asset lifecycle + CMMS. APM: Analytics + prediction + EAM. Modern APM platforms focus on the predictive and prescriptive layers, often integrating with existing EAM/CMMS.

How accurate are predictive maintenance models really?

General

Well-tuned models achieve 85-95% accuracy for common failure modes. Accuracy depends on: data quality, failure history availability, and physics model integration. Expect 6-12 months to reach peak performance.

Cloud vs. on-premise for industrial APM?

General

Cloud is winning. Benefits: faster deployment, automatic updates, better ML capabilities, easier scaling. Edge computing handles latency-sensitive use cases. Hybrid architectures are most common in practice.

What cybersecurity considerations apply to APM?

General

Critical: network segmentation (OT/IT separation), encrypted data transmission, role-based access control, audit logging. Modern platforms are SOC 2 compliant. Work with your CISO from day one.
